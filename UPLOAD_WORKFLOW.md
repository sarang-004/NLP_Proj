# Dashboard resume upload workflow

Run `python app.py`, open `http://127.0.0.1:5000`, and submit up to 10 PDF, DOCX, or TXT resumes against a JD. Supply the JD either by pasting text or uploading a text-based PDF. Both resume and JD files are read from their upload streams in memory (12 MB maximum each). Scanned image-only JD PDFs are not OCR processed. Resume/JD text and evaluation records are not written to project files or retained between requests.

The adapter in `screening/upload_workflow.py` calls `data_layer.structure_resume()` for conservative extraction and `screening.decision_layer.evaluate()`, which calls the existing `run_screening.score()` scorer. It preserves the current component weights and project availability normalization. It adds the submitted full JD as semantic input for upload requests only; existing batch inputs do not set that override.

Skills and minimum experience are detected from the full JD text when their optional override fields are blank. Skill detection matches exact terms in the project's structured-job vocabulary plus common phrase aliases; numeric experience detection recognizes explicit years-of-experience statements (including phrases such as "4+ years developing in Ruby and Java") and uses the lower end of a stated range. The result reports each used criterion and whether it came from the JD or an override. This deterministic extraction does not interpret arbitrary skills outside its vocabulary or resolve nuanced preferred-versus-required language, so review detected criteria and overrides.

Resume date ranges are preserved as separate role records with organization, title, source dates, category, and supporting text. Durations use inclusive calendar months: May through July is 3 months. Overlapping ranges are unioned within each category. The upload's total-professional duration includes dated full-time, part-time, contract/freelance, and internship roles; student leadership, projects, and unclassified roles are displayed separately and excluded. When the JD names a work activity such as backend development, only dated professional or internship roles with matching role or resume evidence contribute. Technology-specific durations still require explicit resume text that ties years to the named technology and activity; a skills-list mention does not establish duration. Date totals do not by themselves establish technology or role relevance, and uncertain records remain labeled for review. Unavailable evidence is not treated as proof that a candidate lacks the qualification.

Names, age, college, and city are not inferred by the upload adapter. Age and experience are only emitted when the existing conservative extractors find explicit evidence. Degree and course evidence are separate in the result, while the model continues to combine them into one score and weighted contribution. Candidate IDs are request-local (`Candidate 1`, etc.) and the response is not saved.

PDF and DOCX support requires the `pypdf` and `python-docx` entries in `requirements.txt`. Install project requirements in the active environment before using those formats. TXT extraction uses UTF-8 with a Windows-1252 fallback.
